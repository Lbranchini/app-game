# 01 — Product Overview

## Pitch

> A tactical turn-based battler where you command teams of three legends — heroes, gods, and historical figures from every mythology in the world — in fast, strategic PvP duels.

Think **chess with superpowers**: each match runs 5–15 minutes, requires turn planning and resource management, and rewards whoever assembles the best team synergy.

## Inspiration

- Turn-based 3v3 browser battlers — random energy generation, cooldowns, action queue.
- Hearthstone — visual clarity of cards and abilities.
- Marvel Snap — short matches, high combo variety.
- Slay the Spire — clear status effects and combo planning.

## Audience

- 16–35 years old.
- Fans of turn-based tactics, mythology, history, and RPGs.
- Casual players who want short sessions on the go but with strategic depth.

## Differentiators

1. **Rich cultural theme:** every legend has flavor text, real origins, and skills faithful to the myths.
2. **Accessibility:** matches fit into a coffee break (≤ 15 min).
3. **Fair F2P:** progression through gameplay; no pay-to-win. Monetization via cosmetics and themed expansions (e.g. "Aztec Pantheon", "Medieval Heroes").
4. **No IP risk:** 100% public-domain figures, infinitely expandable.

## Platforms

- **Web** (first): modern desktop and mobile browsers — Chrome, Safari, Firefox.
- **Native mobile** (later): Android and iOS once the web product is stable.

## Monetization (TBD)

- Cosmetics (alternate skins, avatar borders, ability VFX).
- Seasonal Battle Pass.
- Character expansions (also obtainable through gameplay).
- **Non-goals:** loot boxes, energy gates to play, character paywalls.

## Success metrics (north stars)

- D1 retention ≥ 40%
- D7 retention ≥ 20%
- Average match length: 8–12 min
- Matchmaking ≤ 30s at peak hours
